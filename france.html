South of France

Lavender skies and Riviera light

Provence

Provence is a sensory delight, where the air is fragrant with lavender and rosemary. Explore charming villages like Gordes and Roussillon, wander through vibrant markets in Aix-en-Provence, and visit the historic Palais des Papes in Avignon. The region's stunning landscapes, from the rolling vineyards to the dramatic Calanques, provide a perfect backdrop for outdoor adventures and leisurely picnics.

Côte d'Azur (French Riviera)

The French Riviera is synonymous with glamour and natural beauty. Stroll along the Promenade des Anglais in Nice, explore the medieval streets of Eze, and visit the luxurious city of Cannes. Don't miss the chance to see the glitz and glamour of Monaco, with its famous casino and stunning harbor. The turquoise waters, sun-drenched beaches, and vibrant nightlife make the Côte d'Azur a must-visit destination. Nice serves as the perfect base to explore this glamorous region. Cannes and Saint-Tropez are just a short drive away, offering a taste of the high life with their luxury yachts and upscale boutiques.

Gorges du Verdon

Known as Europe's Grand Canyon, the Gorges du Verdon is a haven for nature lovers and adventure seekers. Hike along the rim for breathtaking views of the turquoise Verdon River below, or try your hand at kayaking or rock climbing in this dramatic landscape. The surrounding villages, such as Moustiers-Sainte-Marie, offer charming spots to relax and enjoy local cuisine after a day of exploration.
