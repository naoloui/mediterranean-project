Italy

Where beauty, history and flavour intertwine

Rome

The Eternal City is a living museum where ancient history meets vibrant modern life. Explore iconic landmarks like the Colosseum, Roman Forum, and Vatican City, then indulge in world-class cuisine at charming trattorias. Stand before the Trevi Fountain and toss a coin to ensure your return to this timeless city.

Florence & Tuscany

Florence, the cradle of the Renaissance, boasts masterpieces by Michelangelo, Leonardo da Vinci, and Botticelli. Wander through the Uffizi Gallery and climb to the top of the Duomo for panoramic views. Beyond Florence, Tuscany's rolling hills are dotted with vineyards, medieval towns, and olive groves. Savor world-renowned wines and traditional Tuscan cuisine in this idyllic countryside.

Veneto

The Veneto region is a treasure trove of art, history, and natural beauty. Venice, with its labyrinth of canals, stunning architecture, and vibrant culture, is a must-visit. Explore St. Mark's Basilica, the Doge's Palace, and take a gondola ride through the serene waterways. Beyond Venice, the Veneto countryside offers charming towns like Verona and Padua, as well as the breathtaking Dolomite Mountains. Lake garda is perfect for relaxation and outdoor activities.

Naples & Amalfi Coast

Naples, the birthplace of pizza, is a vibrant city brimming with history and culture. Explore the historic center, a UNESCO World Heritage site, and visit the nearby ruins of Pompeii. Just south of Naples, the Amalfi Coast offers breathtaking coastal scenery, charming cliffside villages like Positano and Amalfi, and crystal -clear waters. Enjoy fresh seafood and stunning views along this iconic stretch of coastline.

Lombardy

Lombardy is a dynamic region that seamlessly blends cosmopolitan cities with serene lakes and alpine landscapes. Milan, Italy's fashion and financial capital, is home to the stunning Duomo, world-class shopping, and the renowned La Scala opera house. Just a short drive away, Lake Como and Lake Garda offer picturesque settings for relaxation and outdoor activities, surrounded by charming towns and luxurious villas. Bergamo and Brescia add historical depth to the region with their medieval architecture and rich cultural heritage.

Puglia

Puglia, the "heel" of Italy's boot, is a region of stunning coastlines, whitewashed hill towns, and unique architecture. Explore the trulli houses of Alberobello, relax on the pristine beaches of the Salento peninsula, and savor the region's renowned olive oil and seafood cuisine. Puglia's rich history is evident in its ancient churches, baroque architecture in Lecce, and vibrant local traditions.

Sicily

Sicily, the largest Mediterranean island, is a land of rich history, diverse landscapes, and vibrant culture. Explore ancient Greek ruins in Agrigento, the baroque architecture of Noto, and the bustling markets of Palermo. Nature lovers can hike Mount Etna, Europe's highest active volcano, or relax on the island's stunning beaches. Sicilian cuisine, with its blend of Mediterranean flavors, is a highlight not to be missed. Arab conquests, and Norman rule have all left their mark on this fascinating island.

Sardinia

Sardinia is a Mediterranean gem known for its crystal-clear waters, rugged landscapes, and unique cultural heritage. The island boasts some of Europe's most beautiful beaches, such as Costa Smeralda and La Pelosa, perfect for sunbathing and water sports. Explore the ancient Nuragic ruins, hike the Gennargentu mountains, and discover charming villages like Alghero and Bosa. Sardinian cuisine, featuring fresh seafood, pecorino cheese, and traditional breads, offers a delicious taste of the island's rich culinary traditions.
