Turkiye

Where east meets west

Istanbul

Istanbul, a city that straddles two continents, is a vibrant tapestry of history, culture, and modernity. Explore the majestic Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque, and the Topkapi Palace, each echoing tales of empires past. Wander through the bustling Grand Bazaar and Spice Market, where the air is filled with exotic scents and lively chatter. Don't miss a leisurely cruise along the Bosphorus, offering stunning views of the city's skyline.

Cappadocia

Cappadocia is a surreal landscape of fairy chimneys, ancient cave dwellings, and expansive underground cities. Take a hot air balloon ride at sunrise for a breathtaking view of the unique terrain. Explore the Göreme Open-Air Museum, a UNESCO World Heritage site, featuring rock-cut churches adorned with stunning frescoes. Wander through the charming towns of Ürgüp and Avanos, known for their pottery and local crafts. Cappadocia offers an unforgettable blend of natural beauty and rich history.

Izmir (region) & Pamukkale

The Izmir region, with its Aegean coastline, offers a perfect blend of history, culture, and natural beauty. Explore the ancient city of Ephesus, home to the Temple of Artemis and the Library of Celsus. Wander through the charming streets of Alaçatı and Çeşme, known for their vibrant nightlife and beautiful beaches. Just a short drive away, Pamukkale is renowned for its stunning white travertine terraces and thermal waters. Visit the ancient city of Hierapolis, perched above the terraces, to explore well-preserved ruins and a fascinating necropolis. Ephesus is one of the best-preserved ancient cities in the Mediterranean region, offering a glimpse into Roman urban life with its grand amphitheater, intricate mosaics, and the iconic Temple of Artemis, one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World.

Antalya & Turquoise Coast

Antalya, a jewel of the Turquoise Coast, is renowned for its stunning beaches, crystal-clear waters, and rich history. Explore the charming old town (Kaleiçi) with its narrow cobblestone streets, historic architecture, and vibrant markets. Visit the ancient ruins of Perge, Aspendos, and Termessos to delve into the region's fascinating past. The Turquoise Coast offers numerous opportunities for water sports, boat tours, and exploring hidden coves along its picturesque shoreline.

Fethiye & Ölüdeniz

Fethiye, nestled along the Turquoise Coast, is a gateway to stunning natural beauty and ancient history. Explore the picturesque harbor, vibrant markets, and charming old town. Don't miss a visit to the nearby Ölüdeniz, famous for its breathtaking blue lagoon and paragliding opportunities. The region also offers access to the Lycian Way, a scenic hiking trail that winds through ancient ruins, pine forests, and coastal vistas.

Mount Ararat & Eastern Turkiye

Mount Ararat, the highest peak in Turkey, is a majestic landmark steeped in biblical history and natural beauty. The surrounding region of Eastern Turkiye offers rugged landscapes, ancient monasteries, and traditional villages. Explore the Ishak Pasha Palace, a stunning example of Ottoman architecture, and visit the historic city of Kars. Outdoor enthusiasts can enjoy hiking, mountaineering, and wildlife watching in this less-traveled part of Turkey.

Ankara

Ankara, the capital city of Turkey, is a vibrant blend of modernity and history. Explore the impressive Atatürk Mausoleum (Anıtkabir), dedicated to the founder of modern Turkey, Mustafa Kemal Atatürk. Visit the Museum of Anatolian Civilizations to discover artifacts from Turkey's rich past, including Hittite, Phrygian, and Roman relics. Wander through the historic Hamamönü district, known for its well-preserved Ottoman architecture, charming cafes, and artisan shops.

Black sea region

The Black Sea region of Turkey is a lush, green landscape known for its tea plantations, dense forests, and traditional villages. Explore the charming city of Trabzon, home to the stunning Sumela Monastery, perched on a cliffside overlooking the Altındere Valley. Visit the picturesque town of Amasra, with its historic castle and vibrant harbor. The region offers numerous hiking opportunities, including the Kaçkar Mountains, where visitors can experience breathtaking views and diverse flora and fauna.

Adana

Adana, located in southern Turkey, is a vibrant city known for its rich history, delicious cuisine, and lively culture. Explore the impressive Sabancı Central Mosque, one of the largest mosques in Turkey, and stroll along the scenic Seyhan River. Don't miss the historic Taşköprü (Stone Bridge) and the ancient Roman ruins of Anavarza nearby. Adana is also famous for its culinary delights, particularly the iconic Adana kebab, which can be savored at local eateries throughout the city.
