    • Weather

    The Algarve enjoys a Mediterranean climate with hot, dry summers and mild, wet winters. Summer temperatures often reach up to 30°C (86°F), while winter temperatures typically range from 10-15°C (50-59°F). The region receives most of its rainfall between November and February.
