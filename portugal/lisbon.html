    • Weather

    Lisbon enjoys a Mediterranean climate with mild, rainy winters and hot, dry summers. Average temperatures range from 11°C (52°F) in winter to 28°C (82°F) in summer. The city receives around 2,800 hours of sunshine annually, making it a great destination year-round. The wettest months are from November to February, while the summer months of June to September are typically dry and sunny.

    Getting around

    • Trams

      Lisbon's iconic trams are a charming way to explore the city's hilly streets and historic neighborhoods. The vintage yellow trams, such as Tram 28, offer scenic routes through popular areas like Alfama, Baixa, and Bairro Alto. Tickets can be purchased at tram stops or on board.

    • Metro

      Lisbon's metro system is an efficient and affordable way to get around the city. It consists of four lines (Blue, Yellow, Green, and Red) that connect key areas, including the airport, city center, and suburbs. The metro operates from early morning until late at night, with frequent service during peak hours. Tickets can be purchased at stations or via a rechargeable Viva Viagem card.

    • Buses and Trains

      Lisbon has an extensive bus network operated by Carris, covering areas not served by the metro. Additionally, the city's suburban train lines connect Lisbon to nearby towns and attractions, such as Sintra and Cascais. Tickets for buses and trains can be bought at stations or using the Viva Viagem card.

    • Ferries

      Ferries provide a scenic way to cross the Tagus River and reach the southern bank, including the towns of Almada and Seixal. The ferry terminals are located near Cais do Sodré and Terreiro do Paço. Tickets can be purchased at the terminals or on board.

    Day Trips

    • Sintra

      A UNESCO World Heritage site, Sintra is known for its romantic 19th-century architecture, including the colorful Pena Palace, the Moorish Castle, and the Quinta da Regaleira. The town is surrounded by lush hills and offers stunning views of the Atlantic Ocean.

      Cascais

      A charming coastal town located about 30 kilometers west of Lisbon, Cascais is famous for its beautiful beaches, vibrant marina, and lively town center. Visitors can enjoy fresh seafood, explore the historic center, or relax on the sandy shores.

      Óbidos

      A picturesque medieval town surrounded by well-preserved walls, Óbidos is known for its narrow cobblestone streets, whitewashed houses, and charming atmosphere. The town hosts various festivals throughout the year, including a popular chocolate festival.
