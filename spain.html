Spain

Fiery passion meets golden sunsets

Madrid

The vibrant capital of Spain, Madrid is a city that never sleeps. Explore world-class art at the Prado Museum, stroll through the lush Retiro Park, and experience the lively atmosphere of Plaza Mayor. Don't miss the chance to savor traditional tapas and enjoy the city's bustling nightlife.

Barcelona & Catalonia

Barcelona, the cosmopolitan capital of Catalonia, is renowned for its unique blend of Gothic and modernist architecture. Marvel at Gaudí's masterpieces like the Sagrada Família and Park Güell, stroll down La Rambla, and relax on the city's beautiful beaches. The vibrant culture and delicious cuisine make Barcelona a must-visit destination. Catalonia's diverse landscapes, from the Pyrenees to the Costa Brava, offer endless opportunities for adventure and exploration.

Andalusia & Canary Islands

Andalusia, in southern Spain, is a land of rich history and vibrant culture. Explore the stunning Alhambra in Granada, the Mezquita in Córdoba, and the lively streets of Seville. Experience the passion of flamenco dancing, indulge in tapas, and soak up the sun on the Costa del Sol's beautiful beaches. Andalusia's blend of Moorish architecture and Spanish traditions creates an unforgettable experience. Málaga, Cádiz, and Ronda are just a few of the captivating cities to discover in this diverse region. The Canary Islands offer a subtropical paradise with volcanic landscapes, beautiful beaches, and unique flora and fauna. Each island has its own charm, from the lively nightlife of Tenerife to the tranquil beauty of La Gomera.

Valencia & Costa Blanca

Valencia, located on Spain's southeastern coast, is famous for its futuristic City of Arts and Sciences, beautiful beaches, and the annual Fallas festival. Explore the historic old town, enjoy the vibrant nightlife, and savor the region's signature dish, paella. The nearby Costa Blanca offers stunning Mediterranean beaches, charming coastal towns like Alicante and Benidorm, and a variety of outdoor activities.

Ballearic Islands

The Balearic Islands, including Mallorca, Menorca, Ibiza, and Formentera, are a Mediterranean paradise known for their stunning beaches, crystal-clear waters, and vibrant nightlife. Mallorca offers a mix of beautiful beaches, historic towns like Palma, and scenic mountain landscapes. Ibiza is famous for its lively party scene, while Menorca provides a more tranquil escape with its unspoiled beaches and charming villages. Formentera, the smallest island, is perfect for those seeking peace and natural beauty.

San Sebastián & Basque Country

San Sebastián, located in the Basque Country, is renowned for its beautiful beaches, world-class cuisine, and vibrant culture. Stroll along La Concha Beach, explore the charming old town, and indulge in pintxos, the Basque version of tapas. The Basque Country also offers stunning landscapes, from the rugged coastline to the rolling hills and mountains inland. Visit Bilbao to see the iconic Guggenheim Museum and experience the region's unique blend of tradition and modernity.
