Greece

Myths by the sea

Athens & Surroundings

Athens, the cradle of Western civilization, is a city where ancient history meets vibrant modern life. Explore iconic landmarks like the Acropolis, Parthenon, and Ancient Agora, then wander through the charming Plaka neighborhood with its narrow streets and traditional tavernas. Don't miss a visit to the National Archaeological Museum, home to an extensive collection of Greek antiquities.

Santorini

Santorini is the epitome of Greek island beauty, with its iconic white-washed buildings and blue-domed churches set against the backdrop of the Aegean Sea. Explore the charming villages of Fira and Oia, known for their stunning sunsets and vibrant nightlife. Relax on unique volcanic beaches like Red Beach and Kamari, and indulge in local cuisine at cliffside tavernas overlooking the caldera.

Crete

Crete, the largest of the Greek islands, offers a diverse range of experiences from ancient history to stunning natural landscapes. Visit the Palace of Knossos to delve into Minoan civilization, hike the dramatic Samaria Gorge, and relax on beautiful beaches like Elafonissi and Balos. Explore charming towns such as Chania and Rethymno, where Venetian architecture meets vibrant local culture.

Mykonos

Mykonos is renowned for its lively nightlife, beautiful beaches, and charming Cycladic architecture. Stroll through the narrow streets of Mykonos Town (Chora), visit the iconic windmills, and relax on popular beaches like Paradise and Super Paradise. The island also offers excellent dining options, from traditional tavernas to upscale restaurants, making it a favorite destination for both relaxation and entertainment.

Rhodes

Rhodes, known as the "Island of the Knights," boasts a rich history and stunning medieval architecture. Explore the UNESCO-listed Old Town of Rhodes with its cobblestone streets, ancient fortifications, and the Palace of the Grand Master. Relax on beautiful beaches like Tsambika and Lindos, and visit the ancient acropolis of Lindos for breathtaking views over the Aegean Sea.

Delphi & Meteora

Delphi, once considered the center of the world in ancient Greek mythology, is home to the Temple of Apollo and the ancient oracle. Explore the archaeological site and museum to learn about its historical significance. Meteora, with its stunning rock formations and monasteries perched atop cliffs, offers a unique blend of natural beauty and spiritual heritage. Hike through the trails connecting the monasteries for breathtaking views of the surrounding landscape.

Corfu

Corfu, a lush island in the Ionian Sea, is known for its vibrant culture, beautiful beaches, and Venetian architecture. Explore the UNESCO-listed Old Town of Corfu with its narrow alleys, historic fortresses, and lively squares. Relax on stunning beaches like Paleokastritsa and Glyfada, and enjoy local cuisine at seaside tavernas.

Naxos

Naxos, the largest of the Cyclades islands, offers a perfect blend of beautiful beaches, ancient ruins, and traditional villages. Visit the Portara, the iconic marble gate of an unfinished temple dedicated to Apollo, and explore the charming Old Town with its narrow streets and Venetian castle. Relax on pristine beaches like Agios Prokopios and Plaka, and savor local delicacies such as kitron liqueur and cheeses.

Thessaloniki

Thessaloniki, Greece's second-largest city, is a vibrant cultural hub with a rich history. Explore Byzantine walls, Ottoman monuments, and lively markets. Don't miss the White Tower, Aristotelous Square, and the waterfront promenade. Savor the city's renowned culinary scene, blending traditional Greek flavors with modern influences.
